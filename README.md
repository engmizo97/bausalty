# Bausalty (بوصالتي)

> **Holland Code (RIASEC) Personality-to-College-Major Alignment Engine for Saudi Students**  
> *A project under Tahseen AI Group (مجموعة تحسين للذكاء الاصطناعي)*

---

## 📌 Overview

**Bausalty** (Arabic for *"My Compass"*) is an intelligent personality-to-college-major recommendation engine designed specifically for high school and preparatory students in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. 

By leveraging John Holland’s **RIASEC (Holland Code)** career theory—adapted for the Saudi educational landscape and Saudi Vision 2030 human capital development goals—Bausalty guides students toward university majors and career pathways where they are most likely to thrive.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

- **RIASEC Personality Profiling**: Evaluates student interest profiles across six primary traits:
  - **R**ealistic (العلمي / العملي)
  - **I**nvestigative (الاستكشافي / البحثي)
  - **A**rtistic (الفني / الإبداعي)
  - **S**ocial (الاجتماعي / الخدمي)
  - **E**nterprising (القيادي / الريادي)
  - **C**onventional (اللقياسي / التنظيمي)
- **Saudi Higher Education Mapping**: Maps Holland Codes to academic majors offered across Saudi public and private universities (e.g., King Saud University, KFUPM, KAU, KAUST, etc.).
- **Saudi Vision 2030 Alignment**: Highlights majors connected to high-growth national sectors including AI & Tech, Renewable Energy, Tourism & Hospitality, Healthcare, and Financial Services.
- **Tahseen AI Engine Integration**: Powered by Tahseen AI Group's localized machine learning and psychometric scoring algorithms.

---

## 🎯 Target Audience

- **Saudi High School Graduates**: Students navigating post-secondary education decisions.
- **Academic Advisors & Guidance Counselors**: Secondary school counselors assisting students with career discovery.
- **Parents**: Helping families make data-driven education decisions aligned with market demand.
